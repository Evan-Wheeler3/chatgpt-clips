# Storyboard

Shots start on the measured word shown (0.1 s lead). Camera: one motivated move per shot.

| # | shot | starts on | what happens | camera |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | yank | first frame | Already in the seat and in a hurry: he reaches up, grabs the belt, draws it across, yanks. It stops dead; his body jerks, the webbing twangs | Medium, small push in on the yank, a 4-frame bump on the lock |
| 2 | clanks | "Clank" (1) | Three more pulls, each locking on its word: confused, irritated, disbelieving. The belt relaxes and reels back between tries | Slow push in |
| 3 | twist | "Annoying" | He slumps and glares at the belt. Everything holds while the camera travels to the webbing in his fist and the light cools | Push in to the webbing, dip to dark |
| 4 | crash | "In a crash" | Test lab. A sled with a dummy runs in and stops against the barrier; in slow motion the dummy keeps going until the belt takes him | Wide, locked off, bump on impact |
| 5 | tension | "So" | The taut belt: from his shoulder up to the guide loop, down the post to the retractor | Follow the webbing, end pushing in on the retractor |
| 6 | retractor | "Inside" | Cutaway. A slow pull: spool, carrier and inertia disc turn together. On "fast jerk" the spool jumps, the disc lags, the pawl swings into the teeth, the spool stops. The webbing is tugged and does not give | Slow push in, bump on the lock |
| 7 | no-lock | "No lock" | Same sled, lock disabled: webbing streams off the reel and the dummy pitches far forward toward the wheel | Medium, locked off |
| 8 | with-lock | "With" | Identical framing: the belt goes taut, a short lurch, a rebound, held | Same as 7 |
| 9 | closure | "And" (2) | Back in the car: one smooth pull, the tongue clicks into the buckle, hand back on the wheel, a small satisfied look | Gentle push in, hold |
