# Credits and licences

Nothing in this episode was downloaded.

- **Protagonist:** the approved Hold Up protagonist (`characters/hold-up-protagonist/`), unchanged design; seated and arm poses are new.
- **Crash-test dummy:** the same rig drawn without face or clothes, with target markers. A derived prop figure, not a new character design; needs the owner's approval like any new look.

- **Sound:** original synthesis in `tools/audio/synth.mjs`, catalogued in `audio/catalog.json`, CC0-1.0.
- **Lighting reflections:** three.js `RoomEnvironment` (MIT, part of three.js).
- **Caption font:** the Hold Up caption style asks for Avenir Next; where it is missing (Linux) the renderer falls back to Inter (SIL OFL 1.1).
