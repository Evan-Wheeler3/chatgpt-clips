# Master production script: "How does a gas pump know when to stop?"

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 31.8 s (estimated; see `narration.md`).
- **Style:** Hold Up: a stylized 3D world, the flat 2D vector protagonist, burned-in hold-up captions, the approved narrator.
- **Machine-readable storyboard:** `story.json`.
- **Timings:** the times below come from the scratch timeline. In production every cut and cue is anchored to a spoken word, so the edit follows the real take.

**Colour language** (the same in every shot; details in `mechanism-plan.md`):
- **Gasoline:** warm amber `#F2A33A`, glossy, with motion streaks.
- **Air:** cool white-blue `#BFE6FF` wisps.
- **Suction / low pressure:** a faint violet tint `#8C7BFF` in the chamber.
- **Mechanism metal:** neutral grey with a warm rim light.
- **Ghosted outer shell:** pale cyan x-ray, 20–30% opacity.

---

## 1 · `overflow` (hook), 0.00–2.80 s
- **Purpose:** first-second impact. The what-if: the pump doesn't stop.
- **Narration:** "How does a gas pump know when to stop?"
- **Composition:** low three-quarter angle (azimuth 35°, eye at the filler neck). The nozzle is in the filler neck, centre frame. The car's rear quarter panel fills the right half; the driver is at frame-left, mid-ground. Fuel runs down the panel into a puddle across the lower third.
- **Characters/props:** the driver (protagonist), car rear quarter + filler neck, nozzle, pump (soft, background left), pavement.
- **Character animation:** opens on the hand on the nozzle grip, face "surprised". On "know" the driver recoils (`surprised` pose, lean back, free hand up), held 1.2 s.
- **Object animation:** amber fuel gushes from around the spout (a ribbon), sheets down the paint, and the puddle spreads outward. It must read in about 1 s.
- **Camera:** slow push-in, medium → close, eased like a dolly, ending on "stop?". The push builds tension towards the click.
- **Transition out:** hard cut on the CLICK.
- **Captions:** lower band (the action is mid-frame). "How does a gas pump / know when to stop?"
- **SFX/ambience:**
  - station ambience fades in;
  - the fuel gush loop starts 0.5 s before the first word;
  - a splash on "know".
- **Factual notes:**
  - This is an exaggerated what-if; the real shutoff prevents it.
  - No brand names on the pump or nozzle.

## 2 · `click-rewind` (hook contrast), 2.80–4.90 s
- **Purpose:** the approved contrast. A loud CLICK, the overflow snaps back into the filler neck, and the twist lands on the nozzle.
- **Narration:** "It doesn't. The **nozzle** does."
- **Composition:** detail framing on the nozzle in the filler neck (azimuth 20°, slightly higher), with the lever visible. The driver's face peeks in at frame-left.
- **Characters/props:** nozzle, filler neck, the driver (partial).
- **Character animation:** "concerned", then on "nozzle" the driver points at the nozzle (`point`, 1 s).
- **Object animation:**
  - On the cut, the overflow plays **in reverse** for about 0.6 s: the puddle shrinks, the sheet climbs back up, the ribbon is swallowed. A light motion trail, no glitch or VHS effects.
  - The lever drops a few degrees with the click.
- **Camera:** **hold**. The rewind is the motion, and a third push-in in a row would feel mechanical (validator note).
- **Transition in:** hard cut, timed to the CLICK (the click cue is anchored 0.1 s before "It", so the cut sits on it).
- **Captions:** lower. "It doesn't. / The *nozzle* does." Emphasis: nozzle.
- **SFX:**
  - **nozzle click** (+2 dB) on the cut;
  - **rewind swoosh** 60 ms later;
  - the gush stops dead on the click.
- **Factual notes:** the click is used as a sound only; its mechanical source isn't claimed.

## 3 · `tip-hole` (reveal), 4.90–9.20 s
- **Purpose:** curiosity. Not electronic, it's air, and it starts at a tiny hole.
- **Narration:** "And it's not electronic. It's **air**. See this tiny hole near the tip?"
- **Composition:** an x-ray of the filler neck. The car body and filler pipe go pale cyan and translucent; the spout stays solid. The spout tip lands in the frame's middle third, leaving the upper band for captions.
- **Characters/props:** spout tip and filler-pipe cutaway.
- **Object animation:**
  - x-ray dissolve over 0.4 s at the shot start;
  - on "hole", a soft pulsing ring on the sensing opening (not a cartoon arrow).
- **Camera:** push-in, medium → detail, from "And" to "hole", eased like a dolly. It discovers the hole.
- **Transition in:** cut ("into the nozzle").
- **Captions:** **upper band, pinned** (mechanism below). "And it's not electronic. / It's *air.* / See this tiny *hole* near / the tip?"
- **SFX:** a soft x-ray shimmer is optional (not in the library; skip unless synthesized). Ambience drops by 3 dB inside the cutaway.
- **Factual notes:**
  - Opening near the spout tip: verified.
  - "Tiny" is descriptive; no size is claimed.
  - "Not electronic" is flagged as a simplification (see `research.md` row 12).

## 4 · `venturi` (mechanism), 9.20–13.57 s
- **Purpose:** the narrow spot. Fast fuel creates suction.
- **Narration:** "Inside, rushing fuel squeezes through a narrow spot, creating **suction**."
- **Composition:** side cutaway of the nozzle body, the main valve and the venturi throat. Fuel flows left to right.
- **Object animation:**
  - The amber fuel stream speeds up through the throat (streak particles stretch and the lines tighten).
  - On "suction", the throat pulses violet once.
- **Camera:** lateral track to the right along the flow (distance 0.25), from "Inside" to "suction", eased like a dolly. The camera travels with the fuel.
- **Transition in:** cut ("inside the nozzle").
- **Captions:** upper, pinned. Emphasis: suction.
- **SFX:** the fuel-flow bed continues under the cutaway at a lower level (the gush loop, filtered, if feasible).
- **Factual notes:** a venturi at or just below the main valve seat ring matches the patents; the position varies by design.

## 5 · `air-path` (mechanism), 13.57–18.97 s
- **Purpose:** follow the air. From the tip hole, up the sensing tube, into the throat. Fuel keeps flowing while air gets in.
- **Narration:** "That suction pulls air in through the hole, up a thin tube. While air gets in, fuel flows."
- **Composition:** starts tight on the tip hole, then pulls back to reveal the whole tube path, spout to body.
- **Object animation:**
  - Cool white-blue wisps enter the hole and travel up the tube.
  - The tube path glows briefly as the air passes.
  - Fuel keeps streaming in parallel, showing the steady state.
- **Camera:** pullback, detail → close, from "That" to "tube.", eased to settle (azimuth −25°, elevation 10°).
- **Transition in:** cut ("follow the air back to its source").
- **Captions:** upper, pinned. Emphasis: hole.
- **SFX:** an airy suction loop from "pulls" (−4 dB, 2.4 s).
- **Validator note:** SHOT_LONG (5.4 s against a 4.5 s guide) is **accepted**. It's one continuous motivated move along the tube, and cutting it would break the path the viewer is following. To be revisited after the real take.
- **Factual notes:** air is drawn in through the opening while it is exposed (US 2,582,195 claim 1).

## 6 · `covered` (trigger), 18.97–22.47 s
- **Purpose:** the trigger. Fuel rises and covers the hole; the air chokes off.
- **Narration:** "When the tank's full, gas covers the **hole**. No more air."
- **Composition:** close on the spout tip inside the filler pipe. The fuel surface sits below the hole.
- **Object animation:**
  - The fuel level rises and covers the hole on "covers".
  - The air wisps thin out and stop on "No more air".
- **Camera:** **hold**. The stillness is the point: the rising level is the only motion, so the eye goes to it. The validator's STATIC_SHOT warning is accepted for this reason. It only sees the camera, not the planned fuel-level effect.
- **Transition in:** cut (back to the tip).
- **Captions:** upper, pinned. The hole stays visible in the lower half.
- **SFX:** air-stop (the hiss chokes off) on "No".
- **Factual notes:** covering the opening stops the in-flow of air, so the suction builds in the chamber (US 3,646,974, US 2,582,195).

## 7 · `diaphragm` (mechanism), 22.47–25.03 s
- **Purpose:** where the suction goes now.
- **Narration:** "So the suction tugs a flexible diaphragm."
- **Composition:** close → detail on the diaphragm chamber above the body. The chamber volume is visible (cut in half).
- **Object animation:**
  - The chamber tints violet.
  - The diaphragm (a soft dark disc) flexes upward on "tugs".
- **Camera:** push-in, close → detail, from "So" to "diaphragm.", eased out.
- **Transition in:** cut ("where the suction goes now").
- **Captions:** upper, pinned.
- **SFX:** diaphragm flex (soft rubbery thump) on "tugs".
- **Factual notes:** the diaphragm is actuated upward by the suction: verified.

## 8 · `latch` (mechanism), 25.03–28.23 s
- **Purpose:** the chain reaction.
- **Narration:** "That pulls a pin, a latch lets go, the lever drops,"
- **Composition:** the pin, latch balls and plunger in one frame, with the lever pivot at frame-left.
- **Object animation** (one beat per phrase):
  - **"pin":** the latch pin rises with the diaphragm.
  - **"latch":** the balls slip inward and the plunger is freed.
  - **"drops":** the plunger drops, the lever's pivot point goes with it, and the lever swings down.
- **Camera:** track left along the chain, pin → plunger → lever pivot, from "That" to "drops", eased like a dolly.
- **Transition in:** cut ("follow the pin to the latch").
- **Captions:** upper, pinned.
- **SFX:** latch release (a small metal snick) on "latch".
- **Factual notes:**
  - Pin on the diaphragm, balls holding the plunger, lever fulcrum on the plunger: all verified (US 2,582,195, US 3,817,285, US 3,603,359).
  - Simplification: real latches have 3 balls and a return spring; we show two balls in the cut plane.

## 9 · `snap` (payoff), 28.23–30.07 s
- **Purpose:** the payoff. The spring slams the main valve shut.
- **Narration:** "and a spring **snaps** the valve shut."
- **Composition:** detail on the main valve and spring from a new angle (azimuth −40°), so it reads as a fresh, decisive image.
- **Object animation:**
  - The spring extends.
  - The poppet slams onto its seat on "snaps" in 2 frames, with a small camera shake (3 frames, decaying).
  - The amber stream cuts off cleanly.
- **Camera:** hold, plus the impact shake.
- **Transition in:** cut ("on the spring").
- **Captions:** upper, pinned. Emphasis: snaps.
- **SFX:** **nozzle click** (+2 dB) on "snaps", a callback to the hook. The flow bed stops.
- **Factual notes:** the main spring closes the poppet once the lever has lost its pivot (verified).

## 10 · `outro` (payoff), 30.07–32.40 s
- **Purpose:** back outside. A clean stop, an impressed driver, and the memorable last line.
- **Narration:** "All from one tiny **hole**."
- **Composition:** starts close on the nozzle tip (exterior), then pulls back wide to the car, pump and driver. Dry pavement.
- **Character animation:** "happy", then a `thumbs-up` to camera on the second "tiny".
- **Object animation:** the tip hole glints once as the camera leaves it.
- **Camera:** pullback, close → wide (azimuth 30° → 38°, elevation 8° → 14°), eased to settle.
- **Transition in:** cut (back outside).
- **Captions:** lower. "All from one tiny *hole.*"
- **SFX:** the ambience returns to full and fades out at the end.
- **Factual notes:** none new.
