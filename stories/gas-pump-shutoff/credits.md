## What CC BY 4.0 requires of us
Source: creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode, Section 3(a).

1. **Identify the creator(s)** as they ask to be credited, plus any copyright notice supplied with the work.
2. **Link to the material** (the source page), where reasonably practicable.
3. **Name the licence** (CC BY 4.0) and link to it.
4. **Say if we modified it.** All of ours are modified: materials, scale, cut sections, removed parts.
5. **Retain the licence's disclaimer notice** if one was supplied.
6. **Don't imply endorsement** by the creator.
7. **No added technical restrictions.** (n/a: we distribute a video, not the model.)

The licence allows these to be met "in any reasonable manner based on the medium". For a YouTube Short, that means the **video description**, which is where Hold Up keeps credits.

**Commercial use:** CC BY 4.0 permits it. CC0 assets (Poly Haven, and any CC0 car) need no attribution; we credit Poly Haven as a courtesy.
