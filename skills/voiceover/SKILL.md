# Narration
- Aim for conversational energy, precise phrasing, natural emphasis, varied intensity and deliberate micro-pauses.
- Do not clone a real creator's distinctive voice.
- Generate one stable voice identity across the video; vary performance rather than switching speakers arbitrarily.
- Record/capture real spoken audio and obtain real word-level timing from alignment, not a guessed average words-per-second rule.
- Edit script for clarity on first listening, and allow meaningful pauses at reveals rather than blindly maximizing WPM.
- TTS vendor is configurable; do not claim a vendor is connected until verified.
- Review the actual WAV/MP3 alongside a timeline before composing detailed shots.
