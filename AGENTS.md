# Hold Up V2 repository instructions

Read [production instructions](CLAUDE.md) and [architecture](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md) before production work.

For storyboards, shot lists, animatics, camera rigs, character or prop choreography, motion previews and final validation, load [`hold-up-cinematography`](skills/cinematography/SKILL.md) at the start of the relevant stage. Follow its linked standards and record motion review in the existing episode QA notes or handoff. Obtain the motion-pass approval described in [motion QA](docs/MOTION-QA.md) before a full production render.

Work in separate development branches and preserve concurrent work. Keep generated video, caches, local recordings and large source media out of Git.
